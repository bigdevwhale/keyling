# Privacy

Keyling has no network access and no account. Your books, sittings, residents and desk stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. A progress file you save, or a card you share, goes only where you send it.

A photo you pick for your own mug is turned into a small 28×33 pixel print right away; the photo itself isn't kept.
