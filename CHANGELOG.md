## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-13

- My log: every sitting by day, newest first. Tap one to fix its number, mark or unmark a finished chapter, or delete it; coins follow the fix. Open it from «My log ›» in the «What did you do?» sheet on the desk, or from Totals → Books.
- Fixed: the sprint timer and the ghost froze a few seconds in and only moved again after reopening the app. They now count down on their own.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-12

- Everything on the desk is drawn anew: the notebook, the sticky note, the eraser, all five mugs, the plants, the lamp, the cat, the candle and the globe. A photo on the plain mug now wraps round it.
- The desk and the residents' screen are three times finer: smooth wood and light, rounded keycaps with clear legends, and the residents drawn in much finer detail on the desk, at home and in the list.
- The keyboard on the residents' screen is the same as the one on the desk.
- The desk shows at once instead of after a dark pause.
- Fixed: a sprint stopped a few seconds in if you left the desk or switched to another app to write. It now keeps going, and asks how it went when you come back.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10

- A writing desk with a mechanical keyboard: log words (or the total from your editor), and letters fall onto the keys for the resident under the space bar.
- Seven key residents – a dormouse, a heron, an eraser, a frog, an inchworm, an owl and a snail – each fed by its own part of writing: words, chapters, editing, sprints, the plan, nights and days off. Levels, stages and an ability while it lives on the desk.
- Sprints against your best-sprint ghost, typo bugs to catch, dust after a week away.
- Totals: a year goal with pace and forecast, a writing calendar, books, records, a road of milestones and nearly forty awards.
- Share cards for the year, month, week, book, streak, calendar, records, a resident or an award, with a preview.
- The workshop: coins buy desk things (try them on first; make a mug from your photo), keycap sets that wear with use and three recorded switch sounds. Things on the desk can be moved.
- Light and dark themes, English and Russian, words or characters, several books, moving progress to another phone. No ads, no account, offline.
