## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10

- A writing desk with a mechanical keyboard: log words (or the total from your editor), and letters fall onto the keys for the resident under the space bar.
- Seven key residents – a dormouse, a heron, an eraser, a frog, an inchworm, an owl and a snail – each fed by its own part of writing: words, chapters, editing, sprints, the plan, nights and days off. Levels, stages and an ability while it lives on the desk.
- Sprints against your best-sprint ghost, typo bugs to catch, dust after a week away.
- Totals: a year goal with pace and forecast, a writing calendar, books, records, a road of milestones and nearly forty awards.
- Share cards for the year, month, week, book, streak, calendar, records, a resident or an award, with a preview.
- The workshop: coins buy desk things (try them on first; make a mug from your photo), keycap sets that wear with use and three recorded switch sounds. Things on the desk can be moved.
- Light and dark themes, English and Russian, words or characters, several books, moving progress to another phone. No ads, no account, offline.
